Traditional knowledge is relevant to intellectual property assessment involving Ayurveda and traditional medicinal knowledge.

SOURCE_ID: IND_PATENTS_ACT
Under Section 3(p) of the Indian Patents Act, 1970, an invention which is in effect traditional knowledge, or which is an aggregation or duplication of known properties of traditionally known component or components, is excluded from patentability.

SOURCE_ID: TKDL_SOURCE_INFORMATION
Documented traditional knowledge may therefore be relevant when assessing whether an Ayurvedic formulation is already known.

SOURCE_ID: TKDL_SOURCE_INFORMATION
The Traditional Knowledge Digital Library (TKDL) is a database documenting traditional medicinal knowledge and may assist in prior-art assessment relating to Indian traditional systems of medicine.

SOURCE_ID: IND_PATENTS_ACT
A patent application involving an Ayurvedic formulation should be assessed for novelty, inventive step and industrial applicability, together with applicable exclusions under patent law.
